What Is BFR?

The Future of Rehabilitation & Performance

Blood Flow Restriction (BFR) training is an evidence-based technique that uses a specialized tourniquet to partially restrict arterial inflow and fully restrict venous outflow during exercise. This creates a unique metabolic environment that accelerates strength gains, hypertrophy, and recovery — even at low loads.

Research shows BFR can produce strength and muscle gains comparable to heavy-load training, making it a game-changer for post-surgical rehab, injury recovery, aging populations, and athletic performance.
